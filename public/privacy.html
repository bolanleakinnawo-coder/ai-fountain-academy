This policy explains what AI Fountain Academy ("we") collects when you use this website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your full name, email address and a securely hashed password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see your password).
  • Purchases: the course, amount, currency, status and Paystack reference of each payment. Card details are handled by Paystack and never reach us.
  • Learning progress: which lessons you completed and where you stopped in a video.

How we use it

To create and secure your account, give you access to the courses you bought, save your progress, provide support and keep records required for accounting.

Who we share it with

Our service providers: Google Firebase (hosting, accounts, database, storage) and Paystack (payments). We do not sell your data.

Your choices

You can update your name in Account settings, and ask us to delete your account by contacting support.
