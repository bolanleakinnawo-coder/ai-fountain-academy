06Questions

Good to know before you start.

Something else on your mind? Contact us.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. The courses are made for beginners. You'll see every step on screen and can pause or rewatch any lesson.

How do I pay?

Payments go through Paystack, so you can use a card, bank transfer or USSD in naira. Your course unlocks as soon as Paystack confirms the payment.

Can I watch on my phone?

Yes. Log in from any phone, tablet or laptop and your courses and progress are there.

How long do I keep access?

Once you buy a course it stays on your dashboard, and you can rewatch the lessons whenever you like.

Do I pay once or every month?

Once per course. There's no subscription.

What if my payment goes through but the course doesn't unlock?

It usually unlocks within seconds. If it doesn't, keep your Paystack receipt and contact us; we'll check it and fix your access.
